This appendix derives the one-dimensional interband emission (and absorption) integral near the **L point** in gold's Brillouin zone, following the same strategy used in [[A8 - Interband Transitions (Rosei)]] for the X point. The conduction band at L is a **saddle point** (upward curvature along $k_\perp$, downward toward $\Gamma$ along $k_\parallel$), just as at X. Because both critical points share the same saddle topology, the derivation is **structurally identical** to A8 — the only differences are the numerical values of the effective masses and the nonzero energy reference $\mathcal{E}_{0c}^{L}$.

# L-Point Dispersion Relations

Near the L point the bands are approximated as

$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{E}_{v}(\mathbf{k})&=-\mathcal{E}_{0v} - A_{v}k_{\perp}^{2} - B_{v}k_{\parallel}^{2}  &&(\mathcal{E}<-\mathcal{E}_{0v})\\ \\
\mathcal{E}_{c}(\mathbf{k})&= \ \ \mathcal{E}_{0c} + A_{c}k_{\perp}^{2} \boldsymbol{-} B_{c}k_{\parallel}^{2} &&(\mathcal{E}>\mathcal{E}_{0c})
\end{align}
$$

with all coefficients $A_{c}, B_{c}, A_{v}, B_{v} > 0$. The conduction band curves **upward** along $k_\perp$ and **downward** along $k_\parallel$ (toward $\Gamma$), making L a saddle point with the same sign structure as X.

The starting point is the same direct-transition integral

$$
\Gamma_{e}^{\text{cv}}(\hbar\omega) \propto\int\limits_{\text{BZ}} d^{3}k \; f(\mathcal{E}_{c}(\mathbf{k})) \Big[1-f(\mathcal{E}_{v}(\mathbf{k}))\Big] \; \delta\Big(\mathcal{E}_{c}(\mathbf{k}) - \mathcal{E}_{v}(\mathbf{k}) - \hbar\omega  \Big)
$$

and we proceed through the same sequence of changes of variables.

# Cylindrical Symmetry

Identical to the X-point case:

$$
d^{3}k \to 2\pi k_{\perp}\, dk_{\perp}\, dk_{\parallel}
$$

# Linearization of Momenta

Identical substitution $u = k_{\perp}^{2}$, $v = k_{\parallel}^{2}$, with the factor-of-two symmetry accounting for $\pm k_{\parallel}$:

$$
2\pi k_{\perp}\, dk_{\perp}\, dk_{\parallel} \;\to\; \pi \frac{du\,dv}{\sqrt{v}}
$$

In these variables the dispersions become **linear**:

$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{E}_{c}(u,v) &= \mathcal{E}_{0c} + A_{c}u - B_{c}v \\
\mathcal{E}_{v}(u,v) &= -\mathcal{E}_{0v} - A_{v}u - B_{v}v
\end{align}
$$

Note that $u$ and $v$ enter $\mathcal{E}_c$ with **opposite signs**, exactly as at X — this is the signature of the saddle-point topology.

# Energy-Space Transformation

We introduce the same physically motivated variables

$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{E} &\equiv \mathcal{E}_{c}(u,v) &&\implies\quad \mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c} = A_{c}u - B_{c}v \\
\Delta &\equiv \mathcal{E}_{c}(u,v) - \mathcal{E}_{v}(u,v) &&\implies\quad \Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g} = \bar{A}\,u + \bar{B}\,v
\end{align}
$$

where

$$
\mathcal{E}_{g} \equiv \mathcal{E}_{0c} + \mathcal{E}_{0v}, \qquad \bar{A} \equiv A_{c} + A_{v}, \qquad \bar{B} \equiv B_{v} - B_{c}
$$

> **Same as X point:** Because both critical points are saddles with $-B_c v$ in the conduction band, $\bar{B}^{L} = B_{v} - B_{c}$, identical in form to $\bar{B}^{X}$.

## Matrix Form

$$
\begin{pmatrix} \mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c} \\ \Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g} \end{pmatrix}
= \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} A_{c} & -B_{c} \\ \bar{A} & \bar{B} \end{pmatrix}}_{M^{L}}
\begin{pmatrix} u \\ v \end{pmatrix}
$$

The determinant is

$$
D^{L} = \det(M^{L}) = A_{c}\bar{B} + B_{c}\bar{A} = A_{c}B_{v} + B_{c}A_{v}
$$

> **Sign of $D^{L}$:** $D^{L} = A_c B_v + A_v B_c > 0$ always, since all curvature coefficients are positive. This is the same form as $D^{X}$ and avoids the sign complications of the previous (incorrect) convention.

## Inverse Transformation

$$
\begin{pmatrix} u \\ v \end{pmatrix}
= \frac{1}{D^{L}}
\begin{pmatrix} \bar{B} & B_{c} \\ -\bar{A} & A_{c} \end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix} \mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c} \\ \Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g} \end{pmatrix}
$$

giving

$$
\begin{align}
u(\mathcal{E}, \Delta) &= \frac{1}{D^{L}}\Big[\bar{B}(\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c}) + B_{c}(\Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g})\Big] \\[6pt]
v(\mathcal{E}, \Delta) &= \frac{1}{D^{L}}\Big[-\bar{A}(\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c}) + A_{c}(\Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g})\Big]
\end{align}
$$

## Integration Limits

The physical constraints $u \geq 0$ and $v \geq 0$ yield the same limit structure as at X (since $D^{L} > 0$).

From $v \geq 0$:

$$
\mathcal{E} \leq \mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{A_{c}}{A_{c} + A_{v}}(\Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g}) \equiv \mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\Delta)
$$

From $u \geq 0$:

$$
\mathcal{E} \geq \mathcal{E}_{0c} - \frac{B_{c}}{B_{v} - B_{c}}(\Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g}) \equiv \mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\Delta)
$$

> **Same as X point:** $\mathcal{E}_{max}$ comes from $v \geq 0$ and $\mathcal{E}_{min}$ from $u \geq 0$, identical to the X-point case.

## The Measure

Expressing $v$ in terms of $\mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}$:

$$
v(\mathcal{E}, \Delta) = \frac{\bar{A}}{D^{L}}\Big(\mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\Delta) - \mathcal{E}\Big)
$$

which is manifestly non-negative in the allowed range $\mathcal{E} \leq \mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}$. Inserting into the measure:

$$
\pi \frac{du\,dv}{\sqrt{v}} \;\to\; \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{\bar{A}\,D^{L}}} \; \frac{d\Delta\, d\mathcal{E}}{\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\Delta) - \mathcal{E}}}
$$

> **Same topology as X:** The singularity $1/\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max} - \mathcal{E}}$ diverges at the **upper** integration limit, identical to the X point. The $k_{\parallel} = 0$ circle (where $v = 0$) maps to the top of the energy range at both critical points.

# Resolving the Delta Function

Assembling the full integral with the measure and resolving $\delta(\Delta - \hbar\omega)$:

$$
\Gamma_{e}^{\text{cv}}(\hbar\omega) \propto \int\limits_{\text{BZ}} f(\mathcal{E}_{c}) \Big[1-f(\mathcal{E}_{v})\Big] \; \delta(\Delta - \hbar\omega) \; \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{\bar{A}\,D^{L}}} \; \frac{d\Delta\, d\mathcal{E}}{\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\Delta) - \mathcal{E}}}
$$

Setting $\Delta = \hbar\omega$, $\mathcal{E}_{v} = \mathcal{E} - \hbar\omega$:

$$
\boxed{
\Gamma_{e}^{\text{cv},L}(\hbar\omega) = \frac{\pi^{2}|\mu|^{2}}{\hbar\sqrt{\bar{A}\,D^{L}}}
\int\limits_{\mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\hbar\omega)}^{\mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\hbar\omega)}
\frac{f(\mathcal{E})\Big[1-f(\mathcal{E}-\hbar\omega)\Big]}{\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\hbar\omega) - \mathcal{E}}}
\; d\mathcal{E}
}
$$

with

$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\hbar\omega) &= \mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{A_{c}}{A_{c}+A_{v}}\,(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_{g}) \\[4pt]
\mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\hbar\omega) &= \mathcal{E}_{0c} - \frac{B_{c}}{B_{v}-B_{c}}\,(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_{g})
\end{align}
$$

# Comparison with the X-Point Result

With both critical points being saddles ($-B_c v$ in the conduction band), the L-point integral is **structurally identical** to the X-point integral from [[A8 - Interband Transitions (Rosei)]]:

| | X point (A8) | L point (this note) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Conduction band | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} + A_c u - B_c v$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} + A_c u - B_c v$ |
| $\bar{B}$ | $B_v - B_c$ | $B_v - B_c$ |
| $D$ | $A_c B_v + A_v B_c > 0$ | $A_c B_v + A_v B_c > 0$ |
| Singularity | $1/\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max} - \mathcal{E}}$ (upper limit) | $1/\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max} - \mathcal{E}}$ (upper limit) |
| $\mathcal{E}_{min}$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} - \frac{B_c}{B_v - B_c}(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g)$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} - \frac{B_c}{B_v - B_c}(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g)$ |
| $\mathcal{E}_{max}$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{A_c}{A_c + A_v}(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g)$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{A_c}{A_c + A_v}(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g)$ |
| CEDS topology | Hyperboloid (saddle) | Hyperboloid (saddle) |
| JDOS onset | $\propto\sqrt{\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g}$ | $\propto\sqrt{\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g}$ |

The **only differences** between L and X are:
1. Different numerical values of $(A_c, B_c, A_v, B_v)$ from different effective masses.
2. $\mathcal{E}_{0c}^{L} \neq 0$ (whereas $\mathcal{E}_{0c}^{X} = 0$ by convention).
3. Different gap energies $\mathcal{E}_g$.
4. Different multiplicity ($N_L = 8$ vs $N_X = 6$).

# Verification: JDOS at $T = 0$

At zero temperature the Fermi factors become step functions. For $\hbar\omega$ just above threshold, the integral reduces to

$$
J^{L}(\hbar\omega) \propto \int_{\mathcal{E}_{min}}^{\mathcal{E}_{max}} \frac{d\mathcal{E}}{\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max} - \mathcal{E}}} = 2\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max} - \mathcal{E}_{min}} \propto \sqrt{\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g}
$$

recovering the expected $M_0$-type onset behavior (the energy-difference surface $\Delta(u,v)$ is a minimum despite the individual bands being saddles).

# Note on Absorption vs Emission

For **emission** (conduction → valence): the Fermi factor is $f(\mathcal{E})[1-f(\mathcal{E}-\hbar\omega)]$.

For **absorption** ($\varepsilon_2$, valence → conduction): the Fermi factor is $f(\mathcal{E}-\hbar\omega)[1-f(\mathcal{E})]$.

At equilibrium these are related by detailed balance: $\text{emission}/\text{absorption} = e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}$.
