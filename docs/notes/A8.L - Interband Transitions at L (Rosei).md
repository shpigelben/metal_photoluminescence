This appendix derives the one-dimensional interband emission (and absorption) integral near the **L point** in gold's Brillouin zone, following the same strategy used in [[A8 - Interband Transitions (Rosei)]] for the X point. The key difference is that the conduction band at L is a **true minimum** (all positive curvatures), whereas at X it is a **saddle point**. This sign change propagates through every step of the derivation and results in a qualitatively different integrand — the square-root singularity appears at the **lower** integration limit rather than the upper one.

# L-Point Dispersion Relations

Near the L point the bands are approximated as

$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{E}_{v}(\mathbf{k})&=-\mathcal{E}_{0v} - A_{v}k_{\perp}^{2} - B_{v}k_{\parallel}^{2}  &&(\mathcal{E}<-\mathcal{E}_{0v})\\ \\
\mathcal{E}_{c}(\mathbf{k})&= \ \ \mathcal{E}_{0c} + A_{c}k_{\perp}^{2} \boldsymbol{+} B_{c}k_{\parallel}^{2} &&(\mathcal{E}>\mathcal{E}_{0c})
\end{align}
$$

with all coefficients $A_{c}, B_{c}, A_{v}, B_{v} > 0$. The crucial difference from the X point is the **plus sign** before $B_{c}k_{\parallel}^{2}$ in the conduction band: the L-point conduction band is an ellipsoidal minimum rather than a saddle, so the constant-energy-difference surface (CEDS) is a closed ellipsoid.

The starting point is the same direct-transition integral

$$
\Gamma_{e}^{\text{cv}}(\hbar\omega) \propto\int\limits_{\text{BZ}} d^{3}k \; f(\mathcal{E}_{c}(\mathbf{k})) \Big[1-f(\mathcal{E}_{v}(\mathbf{k}))\Big] \; \delta\Big(\mathcal{E}_{c}(\mathbf{k}) - \mathcal{E}_{v}(\mathbf{k}) - \hbar\omega  \Big)
$$

and we proceed through the same sequence of changes of variables.

# Cylindrical Symmetry

Identical to the X-point case:

$$
d^{3}k \to 2\pi k_{\perp}\, dk_{\perp}\, dk_{\parallel}
$$

# Linearization of Momenta

Identical substitution $u = k_{\perp}^{2}$, $v = k_{\parallel}^{2}$, with the factor-of-two symmetry accounting for $\pm k_{\parallel}$:

$$
2\pi k_{\perp}\, dk_{\perp}\, dk_{\parallel} \;\to\; \pi \frac{du\,dv}{\sqrt{v}}
$$

In these variables the dispersions become **linear**:

$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{E}_{c}(u,v) &= \mathcal{E}_{0c} + A_{c}u + B_{c}v \\
\mathcal{E}_{v}(u,v) &= -\mathcal{E}_{0v} - A_{v}u - B_{v}v
\end{align}
$$

Note that both $u$ and $v$ enter with the **same sign** in $\mathcal{E}_{c}$ — this is the signature of the ellipsoidal topology and the origin of all subsequent differences from A8.

# Energy-Space Transformation

We introduce the same physically motivated variables

$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{E} &\equiv \mathcal{E}_{c}(u,v) &&\implies\quad \mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c} = A_{c}u + B_{c}v \\
\Delta &\equiv \mathcal{E}_{c}(u,v) - \mathcal{E}_{v}(u,v) &&\implies\quad \Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g} = \bar{A}\,u + \bar{B}\,v
\end{align}
$$

where

$$
\mathcal{E}_{g} \equiv \mathcal{E}_{0c} + \mathcal{E}_{0v}, \qquad \bar{A} \equiv A_{c} + A_{v}, \qquad \bar{B} \equiv B_{c} + B_{v}
$$

> **Compare with X point:** At X, $\bar{B}^{X} = B_{v} - B_{c}$ because the conduction band has $-B_{c}v$. At L, both bands contribute with the same sign to $\Delta$, giving $\bar{B}^{L} = B_{c} + B_{v}$.

## Matrix Form

$$
\begin{pmatrix} \mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c} \\ \Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g} \end{pmatrix}
= \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} A_{c} & B_{c} \\ \bar{A} & \bar{B} \end{pmatrix}}_{M^{L}}
\begin{pmatrix} u \\ v \end{pmatrix}
$$

The determinant is

$$
D^{L} = \det(M^{L}) = A_{c}\bar{B} - B_{c}\bar{A} = A_{c}B_{v} - B_{c}A_{v}
$$

> **Sign of $D^{L}$:** For gold, using the Christensen & Seraphin (1971) effective masses ($m^{*}_{c\perp} = 0.24$, $m^{*}_{c\parallel} = 0.12$, $m^{*}_{v\perp} = 0.70$, $m^{*}_{v\parallel} = 1.03$ in units of $m_{e}$), one finds $D^{L} = A_{c}B_{v} - B_{c}A_{v} < 0$. This is a consequence of the conduction band being more dispersive along $k_{\parallel}$ than the valence band is along $k_{\perp}$. The negative sign must be tracked carefully through the Jacobian and integration limits.

## Inverse Transformation

$$
\begin{pmatrix} u \\ v \end{pmatrix}
= \frac{1}{D^{L}}
\begin{pmatrix} \bar{B} & -B_{c} \\ -\bar{A} & A_{c} \end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix} \mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c} \\ \Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g} \end{pmatrix}
$$

giving

$$
\begin{align}
u(\mathcal{E}, \Delta) &= \frac{1}{D^{L}}\Big[\bar{B}(\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c}) - B_{c}(\Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g})\Big] \\[6pt]
v(\mathcal{E}, \Delta) &= \frac{1}{D^{L}}\Big[-\bar{A}(\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{0c}) + A_{c}(\Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g})\Big]
\end{align}
$$

## Integration Limits

The physical constraints $u \geq 0$ and $v \geq 0$ now yield limits whose roles are **swapped** compared to the X point because $D^{L} < 0$ flips the inequality direction.

From $v \geq 0$ (recalling $D^{L} < 0$, so dividing flips the sign):

$$
\mathcal{E} \geq \mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{A_{c}}{A_{c} + A_{v}}(\Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g}) \equiv \mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\Delta)
$$

From $u \geq 0$:

$$
\mathcal{E} \leq \mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{B_{c}}{B_{c} + B_{v}}(\Delta - \mathcal{E}_{g}) \equiv \mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\Delta)
$$

> **Compare with X point:** At X, $\mathcal{E}_{max}$ comes from $v \geq 0$ and $\mathcal{E}_{min}$ from $u \geq 0$. At L, the roles are reversed: $\mathcal{E}_{min}$ comes from $v \geq 0$ and $\mathcal{E}_{max}$ from $u \geq 0$.

## The Measure

Expressing $v$ in terms of $\mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}$:

$$
v(\mathcal{E}, \Delta) = \frac{\bar{A}}{|D^{L}|}\Big(\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\Delta)\Big)
$$

which is manifestly non-negative in the allowed range $\mathcal{E} \geq \mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}$. Inserting into the measure (and using $|D^{L}|$ for the Jacobian):

$$
\pi \frac{du\,dv}{\sqrt{v}} \;\to\; \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{\bar{A}\,|D^{L}|}} \; \frac{d\Delta\, d\mathcal{E}}{\sqrt{\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\Delta)}}
$$

> **Key topological signature:** The singularity $1/\sqrt{\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{min}}$ diverges at the **lower** integration limit. At the X point, the corresponding factor is $1/\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max} - \mathcal{E}}$, diverging at the **upper** limit. This reflects the different CEDS topology: at L the $k_{\parallel} = 0$ circle (where $v = 0$) maps to the bottom of the energy range, while at X it maps to the top.

# Resolving the Delta Function

Assembling the full integral with the measure and resolving $\delta(\Delta - \hbar\omega)$:

$$
\Gamma_{e}^{\text{cv}}(\hbar\omega) \propto \int\limits_{\text{BZ}} f(\mathcal{E}_{c}) \Big[1-f(\mathcal{E}_{v})\Big] \; \delta(\Delta - \hbar\omega) \; \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{\bar{A}\,|D^{L}|}} \; \frac{d\Delta\, d\mathcal{E}}{\sqrt{\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\Delta)}}
$$

Setting $\Delta = \hbar\omega$, $\mathcal{E}_{v} = \mathcal{E} - \hbar\omega$:

$$
\boxed{
\Gamma_{e}^{\text{cv},L}(\hbar\omega) = \frac{\pi^{2}|\mu|^{2}}{\hbar\sqrt{\bar{A}\,|D^{L}|}}
\int\limits_{\mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\hbar\omega)}^{\mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\hbar\omega)}
\frac{f(\mathcal{E})\Big[1-f(\mathcal{E}-\hbar\omega)\Big]}{\sqrt{\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\hbar\omega)}}
\; d\mathcal{E}
}
$$

with

$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{E}_{min}^{L}(\hbar\omega) &= \mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{A_{c}}{A_{c}+A_{v}}\,(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_{g}) \\[4pt]
\mathcal{E}_{max}^{L}(\hbar\omega) &= \mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{B_{c}}{B_{c}+B_{v}}\,(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_{g})
\end{align}
$$

# Comparison with the X-Point Result

| | X point (A8) | L point (this note) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Conduction band | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} + A_c u - B_c v$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} + A_c u + B_c v$ |
| $\bar{B}$ | $B_v - B_c$ | $B_c + B_v$ |
| $D$ | $A_c B_v + A_v B_c > 0$ | $A_c B_v - A_v B_c$ (can be $<0$) |
| Singularity | $1/\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max} - \mathcal{E}}$ (upper limit) | $1/\sqrt{\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{min}}$ (lower limit) |
| $\mathcal{E}_{min}$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} - \frac{B_c}{B_v - B_c}(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g)$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{A_c}{A_c + A_v}(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g)$ |
| $\mathcal{E}_{max}$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{A_c}{A_c + A_v}(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g)$ | $\mathcal{E}_{0c} + \frac{B_c}{B_c + B_v}(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g)$ |
| CEDS topology | Hyperboloid (saddle) | Ellipsoid (minimum) |
| JDOS onset | $\propto\sqrt{\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g}$ | $\propto\sqrt{\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g}$ |

Both give the same $\sqrt{\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g}$ JDOS onset (both are $M_0$-type critical points for the energy difference surface with gold's band parameters), but the position of the square-root singularity inside the integrand differs, leading to different spectral shapes once the Fermi factors are included.

# Verification: JDOS at $T = 0$

At zero temperature the Fermi factors become step functions. For $\hbar\omega$ just above threshold, the integral reduces to

$$
J^{L}(\hbar\omega) \propto \int_{\mathcal{E}_{min}}^{\mathcal{E}_{max}} \frac{d\mathcal{E}}{\sqrt{\mathcal{E} - \mathcal{E}_{min}}} = 2\sqrt{\mathcal{E}_{max} - \mathcal{E}_{min}} \propto \sqrt{\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g}
$$

recovering the expected $M_0$ (minimum) critical-point behavior.

# Note on Absorption vs Emission

For **emission** (conduction → valence): the Fermi factor is $f(\mathcal{E})[1-f(\mathcal{E}-\hbar\omega)]$.

For **absorption** ($\varepsilon_2$, valence → conduction): the Fermi factor is $f(\mathcal{E}-\hbar\omega)[1-f(\mathcal{E})]$.

At equilibrium these are related by detailed balance: $\text{emission}/\text{absorption} = e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}$.

> **Erratum for A8:** The $\mathcal{E}_{min}$ formula in the original X-point derivation (A8) contains a likely typo in the denominator. The correct expression from the $u \geq 0$ constraint is $\mathcal{E}_{min}^X = \mathcal{E}_{0c} - \frac{B_c}{B_v - B_c}(\hbar\omega - \mathcal{E}_g)$, with $\bar{B} = B_v - B_c$ in the denominator (not $B_v + B_c$ as currently written).
