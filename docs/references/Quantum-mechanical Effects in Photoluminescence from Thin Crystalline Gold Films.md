# Quantum-mechanical Effects in Photoluminescence from Thin Crystalline Gold Films

![[9 - Bowman.pdf]]

Experimentally and theoretically investigates PL from nanometer-thick crystalline gold films, finding spectral features that cannot be explained by bulk optical properties alone. Demonstrates that quantum confinement modifies the electronic density of states and interband transition matrix elements, producing measurable deviations from bulk PL spectra. Combines DFT calculations with experimental measurements.

**Relevance:** A recent experimental benchmark for interband PL in gold; provides data for comparison once the interband contribution is computed.
