# Principles of Nano-Optics

![[6 - Novotny.pdf]]

The standard graduate-level textbook on nano-optics. Covers spontaneous emission, dipole radiation, optical antennas, near-field optics, and the local density of optical states (LDOS). Derives transition rates for quantum emitters via the interaction of a two-level system with the electromagnetic field.

**Relevance:** Primary source for the spontaneous-emission transition-rate formalism. The derivation in [[A1 - Derivation of the General Emission Integral]] draws from this book. Compare with the Bloch-electron treatment in [[Semiconductor Emission Lecture Slides]].
