# Ultrafast Nonlinear Dynamics of Surface Plasmon Polaritons in Gold Nanowires

![[8 - Marini.pdf]]

Develops a theoretical model for the ultrafast nonlinear optical response of surface plasmon polaritons in gold nanowires, including contributions from both intraband (Drude) and interband transitions to the complex permittivity. The interband susceptibility is modelled via a critical-point (Lorentz-oscillator) approach fitted to the gold dielectric function.

**Relevance:** Provides an alternative frequency-domain perspective on interband transitions in gold; useful for cross-checking permittivity contributions and understanding how intraband and interband terms combine in the optical response.
