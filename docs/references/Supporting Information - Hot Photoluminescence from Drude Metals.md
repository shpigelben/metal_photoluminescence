# Supporting Information — Theory of "Hot" Photoluminescence from Drude Metals

![[2 - Hot_PL_supp.pdf]]

Companion document to [[Theory of Hot Photoluminescence from Drude Metals]]. Contains detailed derivations of the emission integrals, the non-equilibrium Boltzmann solution, and auxiliary analytic results omitted from the main text. Useful for checking intermediate steps in the general emission integral and the thermal-factor expressions.
