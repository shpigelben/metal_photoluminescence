# Assistance of Metal Nanoparticles in Photocatalysis — Nothing More Than a Classical Heat Source

![[4 - faraday_discussion.pdf]]

Challenges the interpretation that metal nanoparticles enhance photocatalysis through hot-electron injection, arguing instead that the enhancement is a classical photothermal effect. Shows that experimental results are quantitatively consistent with local heating alone, without invoking non-thermal carrier chemistry.

**Relevance:** Provides broader context for the Sivan-Dubi group's viewpoint on "hot" carrier physics, motivating the need for an accurate theory of metal PL and non-equilibrium electron distributions.
