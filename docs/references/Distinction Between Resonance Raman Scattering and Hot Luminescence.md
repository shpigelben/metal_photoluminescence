# Distinction Between Resonance Raman Scattering and Hot Luminescence

![[A3 - raman_pl.pdf]]

A classic theoretical paper establishing the conceptual boundary between resonance Raman scattering (coherent, instantaneous) and hot luminescence (incoherent emission following partial relaxation). Derives the selection rules and spectral lineshape differences that allow the two processes to be distinguished experimentally.

**Relevance:** Provides the theoretical justification for treating metal PL as "hot luminescence" (incoherent) rather than Raman scattering, underpinning the incoherent-emission treatment used throughout this project.
