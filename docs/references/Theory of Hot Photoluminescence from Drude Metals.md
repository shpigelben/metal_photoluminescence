# Theory of "Hot" Photoluminescence from Drude Metals

![[1 - Hot_PL_main.pdf]]

**Core reference for this project.** Derives an analytic and semi-analytic theory of photoluminescence from bulk Drude metals under CW illumination, focusing on intraband electronic transitions. The steady-state non-equilibrium electron distribution is obtained from a simplified Boltzmann equation (relaxation-time approximation), and the resulting PL spectrum is evaluated using Fermi's golden rule. Introduces the constant-eDOS and analytic approximations whose accuracy is examined in this project.

**Relevance:** The primary paper this project extends — from intraband to interband transitions, and from CW to pulsed excitation.
