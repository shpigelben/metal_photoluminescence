# Phonon-Assisted Optical Absorption in Silicon from First Principles

![[B3 - electron-phonon.pdf]]

Calculates the phonon-assisted interband optical absorption spectrum of silicon from first principles using the Wannier interpolation formalism. Determines quasiparticle energies, optical transition matrix elements, and electron-phonon coupling constants on fine Brillouin-zone grids. Results are in strong agreement with experiment across a range of temperatures.

**Relevance:** Provides methodological reference for phonon-assisted (indirect) optical transitions — the mechanism that enables intraband PL in bulk metals, where momentum non-conservation is mediated by phonons rather than assumed ad hoc.
