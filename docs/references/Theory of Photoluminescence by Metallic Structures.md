# Theory of Photoluminescence by Metallic Structures

![[11 - Greffet.pdf]]

Presents a general theory of PL from metallic structures that relates the emitted power (for a given frequency, direction, and polarization) to a sum over all electronic transitions weighted by matrix elements, the electronic distribution, and the Green tensor. For the intraband case, derives a closed-form expression depending only on macroscopic quantities, shown to be a generalization of Kirchhoff's law of radiation. Benchmarks against Mooradian's 1969 experimental data.

**Relevance:** A competing and complementary theoretical framework to [[Theory of Hot Photoluminescence from Drude Metals]]; addresses the same open questions from a Green-tensor/macroscopic perspective. Directly relevant to the Kirchhoff's law discussion in [[../notes/concepts/A9 - Kirchhoff's Law Of Radiation]].
