# Supplementary Information — "Hot" Electrons in Metallic Nanostructures

![[5 - Boltzmann Equation.pdf]]

Companion document to [[Hot Electrons in Metallic Nanostructures - Non-thermal Carriers or Heating]]. Contains extended derivations of the Boltzmann equation solution, including the relaxation-time approximation and the explicit separation of the electron distribution into thermal and non-thermal parts. Provides the analytical groundwork directly imported into [[Theory of Hot Photoluminescence from Drude Metals]].
