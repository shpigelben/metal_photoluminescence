# Photoluminescence of Metals

![[10 - Mooridan.pdf]]

The first experimental observation of photoluminescence from metals (gold and copper), demonstrating radiative recombination between conduction-band electrons and holes in the d-bands generated by optical excitation. Measured using an argon-ion laser and Raman spectroscopy techniques at temperatures from 5 to 300 K.

**Relevance:** The landmark paper that established metal PL as a phenomenon. All subsequent theoretical treatments — including [[Theory of Hot Photoluminescence from Drude Metals]] and [[Theory of Photoluminescence by Metallic Structures]] — reference this as the foundational experiment.
