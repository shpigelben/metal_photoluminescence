# Semiconductor Emission Lecture Slides

![[A1 - semiconductor_emission_slides.pdf]]

Unpublished lecture slides covering optical emission in semiconductors via Fermi's golden rule for Bloch electrons. Treats stimulated and spontaneous emission in a multi-band crystal, providing the continuous-metal (Bloch-state) counterpart to the two-level-system approach in [[Principles of Nano-Optics]].

**Relevance:** One of the two foundational derivations of the transition-rate formalism used in [[../notes/concepts/A1 - Derivation of the General Emission Integral]]. The open question of reconciling Novotny's and Farhan's approaches is noted in the project log.
