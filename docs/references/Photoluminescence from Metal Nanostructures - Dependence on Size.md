# Photoluminescence from Metal Nanostructures — Dependence on Size

![[12 - Imon.pdf]]

Addresses long-standing contradictions in the experimental literature on size-dependent PL from metal nanostructures. Reconciles conflicting reports with a simple analytic formula that matches PL measurements across a range of structures and illumination conditions. The formula requires only the local electric field and temperature, identifying these as the key physical quantities governing emission strength and its size dependence.

**Relevance:** Recent output from the Sivan group extending [[Theory of Hot Photoluminescence from Drude Metals]] to nanostructures. Directly relevant to the question of geometry and size effects on metal PL discussed in the project introduction.
