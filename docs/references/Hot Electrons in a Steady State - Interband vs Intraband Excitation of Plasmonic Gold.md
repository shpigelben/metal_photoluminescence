# Hot Electrons in a Steady State: Interband vs Intraband Excitation of Plasmonic Gold

![[B1 - hot_e_steady_state.pdf]]

Experimentally distinguishes interband and intraband contributions to the steady-state non-equilibrium electron distribution in plasmonic gold nanostructures using photon-energy-resolved PL measurements. Finds that interband excitation produces a qualitatively different carrier distribution from the intraband (Drude) channel.

**Relevance:** Direct experimental evidence that interband transitions have a distinct and measurable contribution to the steady-state hot-carrier distribution — the central motivation for extending [[Theory of Hot Photoluminescence from Drude Metals]] to include interband processes.
