# "Hot" Electrons in Metallic Nanostructures — Non-thermal Carriers or Heating?

![[3 - hot_electrons_or_heating.pdf]]

Critically examines the prevailing interpretation of "hot electrons" in illuminated metal nanostructures, arguing that the observed phenomena are better explained by classical Joule heating rather than a genuinely non-thermal carrier distribution. Provides a unified theoretical framework that recovers both the thermal (heated Fermi-Dirac) and small non-thermal correction terms from the Boltzmann equation.

**Relevance:** Establishes the physical picture and language used in [[Theory of Hot Photoluminescence from Drude Metals]]; clarifies what "non-thermal" means in the context of the steady-state distribution.
