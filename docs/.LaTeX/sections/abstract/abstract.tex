\begin{abstract}
In conductors, free electrons play a central role in photoluminescence (PL).
Microscopic calculation of electronic transitions inside the conduction band (intraband transitions) at thermal equilibrium results in the famous Planckian emission [TODO: ref].
Transitions between the conduction and valence band (interband transitions) are shown to negligibly contribute to thermal emissions [TODO: ref?].
When perturbed by an external source however (e.g. CW or pulsed illumination) intraband contribution to emission has been shown to noticeably change [TODO: ref].
In this work we seek to quantify the PL contribution of interband transitions in these non-equilibrium scenarios.
\end{abstract}
